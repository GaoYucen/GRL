# ICLR 2027 submission requirements (official-site snapshot)

- Abstract deadline: 2026-09-18 23:59 AOE.
- Full paper deadline: 2026-09-25 23:59 AOE.
- Submission venue: OpenReview, ICLR 2027 Conference.
- Double blind: author identity must not be revealed in main paper or supplementary material.
- Main-text limit at initial submission: 9 pages.
- References do not count toward the page limit.
- Appendices after the references may be arbitrarily long; reviewers are not required to read them.
- Discussion/rebuttal and camera-ready limit: 10 main-text pages.
- Required: AI use statement; it does not count toward the page limit.
- Recommended: ethics statement when relevant; it does not count toward the page limit (up to 1 page).
- Recommended: paragraph-long reproducibility statement before references; it does not count toward the page limit.
- Code can be submitted as supplementary material and must remain anonymized.
- All authors need up-to-date OpenReview profiles; no new authors may be added after the abstract deadline.
- At least one qualified author per submission must register to review at least 3 papers, unless the submission qualifies for the new-author exemption; authors on 3+ submissions must review at least 6 papers (subject to stated ICLR exemptions).
